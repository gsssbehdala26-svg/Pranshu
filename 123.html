Start with these basics

Three small steps make a big difference.

Use your router’s official app or admin guide to review these settings. Menu names vary by router.

01

Choose strong protection

Use WPA3-Personal when available, or WPA2-AES. Avoid outdated options such as WEP and WPA.

02

Set unique passwords

Use a long, unique Wi-Fi passphrase and change the router’s administrator password from its default.

03

Keep the router updated

Install firmware updates from the manufacturer, and turn off WPS if you do not use it.

Quick check

Review a password you’re considering.
